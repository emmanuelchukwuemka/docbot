The service

MigraTech is a migration concierge service. We help you understand which migration pathways you may be eligible for, collect and review supporting documents, and connect you with a specialist for guidance through your application. We are not a government agency and do not process visas or immigration applications ourselves — decisions on any application are made by the relevant government or institution.

MigraTech does not guarantee that you will be approved for any visa, work permit, study place, or other migration outcome. Your responsibilities
